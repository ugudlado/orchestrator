# content-draft metrics

## no_fabricated_evidence

Every statistic, date, quote, name, and citation traces to the brief or the
outline. An unsupported claim must carry a `[NEEDS SOURCE: …]` marker. One
invented source fails this metric outright — fluent prose does not offset it.

## outline_fully_executed

Every section the outline names is present, in order, as finished prose.
Dropped, merged, or reordered sections fail unless recorded under "Outline
deviations" with a reason.

## complete_not_sketched

The draft is the whole piece. Placeholder text, "…and so on", bracketed TODOs
other than `[NEEDS SOURCE]`, or a section that summarizes what it would say
instead of saying it all fail.

## format_and_length_honored

Length and format match the brief, or the draft states the assumption it chose
when the brief was silent — and then holds to it.

## no_meta_commentary

The article body contains no references to being AI-generated, to the drafting
process, or to the instructions being followed.
