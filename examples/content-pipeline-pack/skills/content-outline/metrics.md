# content-outline metrics

Prose rubrics an LLM judge scores the step's output against. Each is pass/fail
with a one-line justification.

## angle_is_a_claim

The outline opens with a single sentence that makes a specific claim the piece
will defend. A restatement of the topic ("this article covers X"), a question,
or a list of themes fails.

## sections_have_purpose

Every section heading is followed by a sentence saying what that section
establishes. A bare heading list fails. A section whose purpose sentence merely
repeats the heading fails.

## gaps_named_not_filled

Claims the brief does not support appear in an Evidence gaps list rather than
being satisfied with invented specifics. Any concrete statistic, date, quote,
or citation that does not trace to the brief is a fail, however plausible.

## brief_constraints_carried

Audience, format, and length from the brief appear in the outline. When the
brief omits one, the outline states the assumption it chose instead of
silently picking one.

## structure_only

The deliverable is structure. Drafted prose sections in place of an outline
fail even when the prose is good.
