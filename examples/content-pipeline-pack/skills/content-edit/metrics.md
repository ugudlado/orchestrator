# content-edit metrics

## gate_fires_on_evidence

`edit_result` is `rejected` whenever an unresolved `[NEEDS SOURCE]` marker
survives, a required outline section is missing, or the piece contradicts the
brief's audience or format. Approving past any of these fails.

## gate_not_taste_driven

Rejection reasons are objective and checkable. Rejecting because the editor
would have made different stylistic choices, with no brief or outline
violation behind it, fails.

## sources_not_invented_to_pass

A `[NEEDS SOURCE]` marker is never cleared by supplying an unverified source.
Rejecting is the correct resolution when the source cannot be confirmed.

## argument_preserved

Edits improve clarity without changing the claims the draft makes. A rewrite
that alters or softens the angle fails.

## constraints_reported

Each brief constraint (audience, format, length, angle) is explicitly recorded
as met or missed, and blocking reasons on rejection are specific enough for the
draft step to act on without guessing.
